Research peptides: what they are and where to find them

In-depth guides to commonly searched compounds: what each one is, what to look for on a certificate of analysis, and links to suppliers we list. For laboratory research only. Supplier links on these pages are affiliate links; see our disclosure.

Synthetic pentadecapeptide

BPC-157

A 15-amino-acid synthetic peptide derived from a sequence found in human gastric juice protein, studied almost entirely in rodent models.
Thymosin beta-4 related peptide

TB-500

A research peptide sold under the name of its parent protein, thymosin beta-4. What a vendor means by TB-500 is not standardized.
Copper-binding tripeptide

GHK-Cu

A naturally occurring tripeptide that binds copper, widely used in cosmetic ingredient research and studied in cell and tissue models.
GHRH analog

Tesamorelin

A modified 44-amino-acid GHRH analog. A pharmaceutical version exists as a prescription drug; research-grade material is a separate, unregulated supply.
Growth hormone secretagogue

Ipamorelin

A synthetic pentapeptide that acts on the ghrelin receptor, often described as more selective than older secretagogues in preclinical work.
GHRH analog

CJC-1295

A GHRH analog sold in two chemically different forms under similar names. Confirming which one you are buying is the main task.
Mitochondrial-derived peptide

MOTS-c

A 16-amino-acid peptide encoded in mitochondrial DNA, studied in metabolism and aging research in cell and animal models.
Coenzyme (not a peptide)

NAD+

NAD+ is a coenzyme, not a peptide. It is listed alongside peptides by most vendors, so it appears in this directory for comparison.
ACTH-derived heptapeptide

Semax

A synthetic analog of an ACTH fragment with a stabilizing Pro-Gly-Pro tail, developed and studied mainly in Russia.
Tuftsin-derived heptapeptide

Selank

A synthetic analog of the immune peptide tuftsin with a stabilizing Pro-Gly-Pro tail, studied in Russian laboratories.
alpha-MSH fragment tripeptide

KPV

A tripeptide fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone, studied in cell and animal models of inflammation.
Mitochondria-targeted tetrapeptide

SS-31

A small synthetic peptide that concentrates at the inner mitochondrial membrane. A pharmaceutical form, elamipretide, has been in clinical development.

The batch-record checklist for any peptide

Whichever compound you are looking at, these are the documents worth having before you trust a vial:

  • A COA dated and numbered for the batch printed on your vial, not a generic or reused file.
  • HPLC purity with the trace shown, and mass-spec confirming identity.
  • The testing lab's name and a way to verify the report independently.
  • Fill amount, counter-ion and storage guidance on the label or listing.
