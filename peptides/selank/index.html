Selank: research overview and suppliers

A synthetic analog of the immune peptide tuftsin with a stabilizing Pro-Gly-Pro tail, studied in Russian laboratories.

Also known as: Tuftsin analog. For laboratory research use only.

What it is

  • Length: 7 amino acids
  • Sequence: Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro
  • Parent: Tuftsin (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg)
  • Typical form: Lyophilized powder

Selank extends the four-amino-acid tuftsin sequence with Pro-Gly-Pro to improve stability. Published work is concentrated in a few Russian research groups, and it is not an approved drug in the US.

As with Semax, vendors list both vials and sprays. For comparison shopping, make sure you are looking at the same format and content.

Batch-record checklist

Before relying on any vial, check the documentation. For Selank specifically:

  • COA mass should reflect the 7-mer, about 751 Da.
  • Compare vial size across vendors, as listed amounts differ.
  • Confirm whether any acetylated form is being sold.

For every compound:

  • A COA dated and numbered for the batch printed on your vial, not a generic or reused file.
  • HPLC purity with the trace shown, and mass-spec confirming identity.
  • The testing lab's name and a way to verify the report independently.
  • Fill amount, counter-ion and storage guidance on the label or listing.
