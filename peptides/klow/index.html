What it is

KLOW at a glance

KLOW is not a single peptide. It is a supplier-coined name for a pre-mixed blend that combines four separately studied compounds in one vial: GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500, and KPV.

The most common published ratio is 50mg GHK-Cu, 10mg BPC-157, 10mg TB-500, and 10mg KPV for an 80mg total blend, though suppliers can vary the exact milligram split, so the label on any given listing should always be checked directly.

Research context

Where current research places it

Skin, collagen, and wound-healing research (from GHK-Cu), tissue-repair research (from BPC-157 and TB-500), and inflammatory-response research (from KPV).

Preclinical for all four ingredients individually; the combined blend itself has no dedicated trial record.

What research has looked at

Because KLOW is a named combination rather than a single molecule, the relevant research sits with its four ingredients individually: GHK-Cu's role in collagen and extracellular matrix biology, BPC-157 and TB-500's roles in tissue-repair and angiogenesis models, and KPV's role in inflammatory-response research.

What each ingredient contributes

Reading a blend as its parts, not as one compound

GHK-Cu is a copper-binding tripeptide studied for roles in collagen synthesis, extracellular matrix remodeling, and antioxidant activity, which is why it anchors the skin-related positioning in this blend.

BPC-157 and TB-500 (thymosin beta-4) are both studied in tissue-repair and wound-healing models, including tendon, ligament, and gastrointestinal research for BPC-157, and angiogenesis and actin-binding research for TB-500.

KPV is a tripeptide derived from the C-terminus of alpha-MSH, studied for anti-inflammatory activity, which is the reason it is added to this blend alongside the repair-focused peptides.

Because the blend combines four independently studied peptides rather than testing the mixture itself, a reader should evaluate the documentation for each ingredient separately rather than treating "KLOW" as a single research-backed entity.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the main questions that come up on this page.

What is KLOW?

KLOW is a supplier-branded name for a peptide blend that combines GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500, and KPV in one vial, most commonly formulated as 50mg GHK-Cu, 10mg BPC-157, 10mg TB-500, and 10mg KPV (an 80mg total blend). It is not a single studied compound; it is a mixture of four separately studied peptides.

What research areas is it linked to?

Each ingredient carries its own research base: GHK-Cu is linked to skin, collagen, and wound-healing research; BPC-157 and TB-500 are linked to tissue-repair research; KPV is linked to inflammatory-response research. There is little to no published literature testing the specific combined formula itself.

How is KLOW different from GLOW?

KLOW is essentially GLOW (GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500) with KPV added as a fourth ingredient, which is why the two names are often listed side by side by the same suppliers.

Has the KLOW combination itself been studied?

Not as a combined formula. The published research covers each of the four ingredients individually rather than the specific pre-mixed blend sold under this name.

What should stand out when comparing supplier pages?

Look for a supplier page that lists the exact milligram breakdown of all four ingredients, provides a batch-specific COA covering the finished blend, and avoids treating the combination as if it had its own dedicated clinical evidence.
