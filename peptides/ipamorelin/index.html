Ipamorelin: research overview and suppliers

A synthetic pentapeptide that acts on the ghrelin receptor, often described as more selective than older secretagogues in preclinical work.

Also known as: NNC 26-0161. For laboratory research use only.

What it is

  • Length: 5 amino acids
  • Class: Ghrelin receptor agonist (GHRP)
  • Approx. mass: about 712 Da
  • Typical form: Lyophilized powder

Ipamorelin is a pentapeptide containing non-natural amino acids, developed as a selective growth hormone secretagogue. It was studied in early clinical work but never became an approved product.

As a short peptide it is straightforward to synthesize, so purity differences between vendors tend to show up in the details: residual solvents, counter-ion and fill accuracy.

Batch-record checklist

Before relying on any vial, check the documentation. For Ipamorelin specifically:

  • Mass-spec should show about 712 Da.
  • Because it has non-natural residues, make sure the COA is for ipamorelin and not a related secretagogue.
  • Check whether it is sold alone or as a blend with CJC-1295; the listing names should be clear.

For every compound:

  • A COA dated and numbered for the batch printed on your vial, not a generic or reused file.
  • HPLC purity with the trace shown, and mass-spec confirming identity.
  • The testing lab's name and a way to verify the report independently.
  • Fill amount, counter-ion and storage guidance on the label or listing.
