KPV: research overview and suppliers

A tripeptide fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone, studied in cell and animal models of inflammation.

Also known as: Lys-Pro-Val, alpha-MSH (11-13). For laboratory research use only.

What it is

  • Length: 3 amino acids
  • Sequence: Lys-Pro-Val
  • Origin: C-terminal fragment of alpha-MSH
  • Approx. mass: about 342 Da

KPV is the C-terminal tripeptide of alpha-MSH. Published work is mainly cell-culture and animal research on inflammatory signaling, including studies in intestinal and skin models.

Its small size makes it easy to synthesize, so the useful comparisons between vendors are purity percentage, counter-ion and whether the COA is batch-specific.

Batch-record checklist

Before relying on any vial, check the documentation. For KPV specifically:

  • Mass-spec should be near 342 Da.
  • For a tripeptide, a single clean HPLC peak should be easy to achieve; treat extra peaks as a flag.
  • Check that the COA is dated and carries a batch number matching the vial.

For every compound:

  • A COA dated and numbered for the batch printed on your vial, not a generic or reused file.
  • HPLC purity with the trace shown, and mass-spec confirming identity.
  • The testing lab's name and a way to verify the report independently.
  • Fill amount, counter-ion and storage guidance on the label or listing.
