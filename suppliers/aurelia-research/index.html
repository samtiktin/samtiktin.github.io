Supplier review

Aurelia Research review

By the PeptideSuppliers.org editorial team · How we review

Aurelia Research is a new UK and US research-peptide supplier operated by ARP Innovations Ltd, a company incorporated in July 2026. Its strongest feature is documentation: a public COA library with a batch number on each certificate, a lookup page that shows the laboratory, test date, measured purity, and measured content, and a link to the laboratory’s own verification portal. Its main weakness is that everything is very new, and prices and payment methods were harder to pin down.

Why it stands out

A batch-level COA library you can look up

Most suppliers say “third-party tested” and stop. Aurelia publishes a certificate for each batch, tells you which laboratory tested it, and reports measured figures rather than round numbers. The batch number printed on a vial is meant to open the same record.

  • 14 batches in the public COA library, with purities between 99.23% and 99.91%
  • Batch lookup shows laboratory, test date, report reference, purity, and measured content
  • Each record links to the laboratory’s own verification portal with an access code
  • The release standard says every batch is also screened for fentanyl, with the method and cutoff stated
  • Separate UK and US stores with local stock stated

Testing and COAs in detail

What the library shows and how the lookup works.

What is published

14 batches, one per product

The COA Library lists certificates for the BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, AHK-Cu, NAD+, MOTS-C, SS-31, Tesamorelin, Cagrilintide, Melanotan II, GLOW, KLOW, BPC-157 + TB-500, and CJC-1295 (No DAC) + Ipamorelin products. Each entry gives a purity figure, a batch number, and a PDF. Two catalog items, AR-RTA and AR-TRZ, appear in the library without a certificate (“Select strength”), so we could not see results for them.

A sample lookup

BPC-157 10mg, batch 08233

The lookup page for this batch shows ILS Laboratories as the laboratory, a test date of 7 October 2026, a report reference, identity confirmed by HPLC, purity 99.84% by HPLC, content of 10.39 mg (103.9% of the 10 mg label), and a fentanyl screen of “not detected” by immunoassay with a 50 ng/mL cutoff. It says the results apply to the sample described in that report.

Checking it

Laboratory portal link

Each record carries an access code and a link to ILS Laboratories’ own verification portal. We loaded the link but the portal renders its content with JavaScript, so we could not read the certificate there, and we have not otherwise confirmed it with the laboratory. The PDF on Aurelia’s site is an image, so we could not extract its text. Try the portal link yourself before relying on it.

The standard

Aurelia Analytical Release Standard

The Release Standard page says vials are picked at random from each batch and tested for identity (HPLC against a reference standard), purity, content per vial, and a fentanyl screen, and that no batch is released unless it meets the standard. It also states that testing describes the sample tested and does not establish safety or suitability for any use. That is the honest framing, and it matches how we read these reports.

How to use this

A practical check before ordering

  • Find your product in the COA Library and note the batch number
  • Open the lookup and the laboratory’s verification link, and confirm the access code and report reference
  • Note the test date, and ask whether the batch you are buying is the one tested
  • When the vial arrives, match its batch number to the record

Check at checkout

Prices were not consistent across pages when we checked. The US BPC-157 10mg product page showed $59.99 (with 10% off three vials and 15% off five), while the US shop page and home page showed $49.99; the US GLOW blend showed $109.99 on the shop page and $144.99 in a related-products list. Shipping and policies

Dispatch, delivery, returns, and eligibility.

Dispatch

UK and US stock

The site states local stock in both markets and shows a free tracked delivery offer on US orders of $150 or more. On the US product page, orders placed before 12 noon Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Methods, costs, and estimates are shown at checkout, and the Terms say delivery is within the market you order from (UK or US) and that estimates are not guarantees.

Returns and refunds

Statutory rights in the UK, narrower in the US

Change-of-mind returns follow UK statutory cancellation rights, and must be unopened, sealed, and in original packaging, with return shipping at the buyer’s cost. For US orders, change-of-mind returns are not accepted unless law requires them. Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items are put right with a replacement, refund, or other remedy, and refunds go to the original payment method.

Eligibility

18+ in the UK, 21+ in the US

Visitors must confirm their age and that they will use the products for in vitro research only before entering the site. The Terms state that laws on purchase and import differ by territory and that buyers are responsible for compliance.

Payment methods

Not visible before checkout

The Terms say payment methods are shown at checkout. We did not go through checkout, so we cannot say what is accepted. Card payments allow disputes; bank transfers, crypto, and peer-to-peer apps usually do not, so check the options before you fill the basket.

Editorial take

Strong batch documentation from a brand-new company

Aurelia Research does the thing most suppliers skip: it ties a certificate to a batch number, names the laboratory and test date, reports measured content, and points you to the laboratory’s own portal. The company details check out against Companies House. What keeps this from a higher rating is age and consistency: the business is a few months old, two items have no certificate, and prices varied by page. We are rating documentation and buyer risk, not product quality, because we have not tested product.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

Worth considering if you look up the batch for your product and confirm it with the laboratory first. Check the price in your basket and the payment options before you order.
