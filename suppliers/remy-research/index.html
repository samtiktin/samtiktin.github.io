Remy Research review

By the PeptideSuppliers.org editorial team · How we review

Remy Research is a Dubai-based research-peptide supplier that launched in 2026 and delivers within the United Arab Emirates. It sells pre-filled pens and lyophilised vials from four brands, and it publishes 11 batch reports from two third-party laboratories, Janoshik Analytical and METS Lab. Its strengths are fast local delivery and report lookups for a subset of products. Its main cautions are its age, how little of the catalog has a published report, and the dosing-oriented tools on its site.

Testing and reports in detail

What is published and what is not.

What is published

11 batches, two laboratories

The Lab Results page lists 11 batches and 20 reports. Janoshik-tested batches include retatrutide pens and vials and a PT-141 pen. METS Lab, a laboratory in Ajman, UAE, tested a TB-500 pen, a GLOW pen, and vials of tesamorelin, BPC-157, GHK-Cu and a BPC-157 plus TB-500 blend. Published HPLC purities range from 99.26% to 99.87%.

A fuller example

Retatrutide 40mg vial, batch RT-40-V-001

The site shows 99.692% HPLC purity with a 49.80 mg assay, plus a microbial count pass, endotoxin under 1 EU per vial, heavy metals not detected, and TFA not detected, each as a separate report with its own verification key. That is a more complete panel than most suppliers publish. A different batch, RETP002, has only a purity and concentration report, and the site says so.

Checking it

Look up the key yourself

Each Janoshik report carries a task number and key. We did not look them up. Do so on the laboratory’s own site rather than relying on the supplier’s copy, and match the batch number to the one on your product.

The gap

Most products have no published report

The shop lists 78 products on the home page (the Brand Facts page says 84 records), including 47 Genovare pens. The 11 published batches cover a small share of that. For other vials, the site says purity is documented at over 99% HPLC with lot-specific reports available on request, and for some items, such as the retatrutide and cagrilintide pen, it says an independent report is pending. Ask for the report for your exact product before you pay.

How to use this

A practical check before ordering

  • Find your product on the Lab Results page and note the batch number
  • If there is no report for it, ask for the batch report in writing before paying
  • Verify the task number and key on the laboratory’s own site
  • When the product arrives, match its batch number to the record

Catalog and brands

What was listed when we checked on October 27, 2026.

Catalog

Four brands, pens and vials

Remy Research resells products from four brands: ProPeptide, Remedium Research, ZPHC and Genovare. The range includes retatrutide in many formats, BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, SS-31, MOTS-c, tesamorelin, CJC-1295 with ipamorelin, GLOW, PT-141 and others, plus bacteriostatic water. Pens make up the majority of products.

Observed prices

In AED, with multi-pack tiers

The home page says prices start from AED 165. Examples shown on the home page include a TB-500 20mg pen at AED 550 and a PT-141 20mg pen at AED 400, with lower per-unit prices at two and five units. The minimum order is AED 400. Prices change, so check the basket.

Prescription-type products

Hormones and an investigational drug

The catalog also lists human growth hormone, HCG, HMG and IGF-1 LR3 pens, and retatrutide, which the site itself says is investigational and not approved. These are sold as research materials. Laws on buying, importing and possessing them differ by country and by emirate, and buyers are responsible for compliance.

Delivery and policies

Dispatch, payment, refunds, and eligibility.

Delivery

UAE only, with cut-off times

Dubai orders placed before 4 PM are delivered the same day. Other emirates receive next-day delivery for orders before noon and within two days after that, Saturday orders outside Dubai arrive Monday, and Sunday delivery is not available outside Dubai. There is a flat AED 50 fee under AED 1,000 and free delivery above it. The site says it delivers within the UAE only.

Payment

Card link, USDT, or Dubai cash on delivery

Cards are not taken at the door. The team sends a payment link for card payments, USDT is accepted, and cash on delivery is offered for Dubai orders of ProPeptide and Remedium products only. Cards allow disputes; USDT and cash generally do not, so consider which method you use.

Refunds

Full refund only before dispatch

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund before dispatch. After dispatch there are no change-of-mind returns, even for unopened products, because the supplier says it cannot verify the cold-chain. Damaged, incorrect or misdescribed orders should be reported within about 48 hours with photographs to orders@remyresearch.com, and confirmed problems are replaced or refunded.

Ordering

WhatsApp, Telegram or the site

The site pushes WhatsApp and Telegram as the fastest way to order and lists an official-channel verification page. Confirm you are talking to the real account, as impersonators are common in this market.

Contact

Email, WhatsApp and Telegram

The site lists orders@remyresearch.com, a WhatsApp number, and a Telegram account, plus a contact-verification page. We did not test response times.

Research framing

In-vitro research only, but see the tools

Pages state that products are for in-vitro laboratory research only and not for human or veterinary use. The site also offers a Pen Planner, a pen clicks-to-milligram converter and a retatrutide dosage guide. Those tools are aimed at dosing, which sits awkwardly with the research-only position. Nothing on this page is dosing or usage guidance.

Very new

Launched in 2026

The supplier is weeks old by its own account, so there is no meaningful order history to lean on.

Carried-over reviews

15 reviews from an earlier brand

The home page shows 15 reviews, all five stars. The site says they came from customers of Remy Peptides, were first shown on that site in June 2026, and were carried over in October. They cover service and delivery, not research results. Treat them as the brand’s own testimonials, not independent ratings.

Limited reports

Most products are unreported

Eleven batches cannot cover a catalog of roughly 80 products across four brands. A report applies to the batch tested, not to every vial.

Inconsistent details

Counts and dates differ

The product count (78 versus 84) and the stated trading date differed between pages. Small, but worth confirming.

Dosing-oriented tools

Pen planner and clicks converter

These tools suggest human use, contrary to the research-only wording. Consider the legal and safety implications where you live.

Editorial take

Good report library for what it covers, but new and thin on coverage

Remy Research does publish verifiable third-party reports, including fuller panels than most suppliers, and it states its policies clearly. What holds back a confident view is that it is brand new, the reports cover a small share of its products, and its site includes dosing-style tools that undercut its research-only framing. We are not rating it, because we have not tested product and there is no history.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

Worth considering for UAE buyers who check the batch report for their exact product first, verify it on the laboratory’s site, and choose a payment method with dispute options. This is a sponsored listing; weigh it accordingly.
