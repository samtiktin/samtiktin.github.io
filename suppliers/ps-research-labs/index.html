PS Research Labs review

PS Research Labs (trading as PS Labs) is a UK-registered supplier with a small catalog of common research compounds priced in GBP. Since this review was first published, PS Labs has added a "Supplier certificate of analysis" link to individual product pages: every product checked for this update (BPC-157, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide, and AHK-Cu) now links out to a Certificate of Analysis, most pointing to an independent Janoshik verification page rather than only a supplier-hosted file. Combined with the company's real UK registration, this is a meaningful improvement over the storefront's earlier documentation posture.

Why it stands out

Verifiable UK Registration, Now With Published COAs

PS Research Labs Ltd lists a Companies House-style registration number (17327405) and a registered office address in Kington, Herefordshire, in its Terms of Service — details a reader can independently check. Product pages now also include a "Supplier certificate of analysis" link; every product checked for this update linked out to a real COA, mostly hosted on the independent Janoshik verification platform.

  • Prices shown publicly in GBP with no account required to browse
  • UK dispatch in 1-3 business days; international shipping available with customer responsible for customs

Review summary

Where it looks strongest

The registered company details remain the clearest baseline positive — a real UK Ltd entity with a checkable registration number — and product pages now back that up with an actual, clickable Certificate of Analysis link rather than only a promise of documentation.

Trust signals

What improved

Every product checked for this update (BPC-157, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide, AHK-Cu) now shows a "Supplier certificate of analysis" link at the bottom of the page. Three of the four linked to an independent Janoshik verification page; one (Retatrutide) linked to a self-hosted PDF on the store's own CDN rather than an independent lab site.

Potential drawback

What to double-check

The specifications accordion on each product page still reads "Documentation: Batch documentation available where applicable," which is stale copy now that a COA is actually linked below it. Confirm the linked COA's batch/lot number matches what ships with your order, and note that not every COA is independently hosted.

Ideal buyer

Who this page points toward

Researchers who want a verifiable UK business registration and are now also able to check a per-product COA before ordering, rather than requesting it directly.

Catalog and documentation

What's listed

The catalog covers a handful of common compounds — Retatrutide, Tirzepatide, BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, and MOTS-C were visible at the time of this review — each with a "Specifications" accordion listing strength, form, storage guidance, and a "Supplier certificate of analysis" link near the bottom of the page.

Where COAs live

Published per product, mostly independently verified

There's no standalone COA library page, but every product page checked for this update (BPC-157, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide, AHK-Cu) carried a "Supplier certificate of analysis" link. Most pointed to verify.janoshik.com, an independent testing verification platform; Retatrutide's linked to a self-hosted PDF instead.

Company details are real, but verify independently

Registration number 17327405

PS Research Labs Ltd's Terms of Service list a registered office at 61a Bridge Street, Kington, HR5 3DJ, United Kingdom. A registered office is a legal requirement for a UK Ltd company and can be a mail-forwarding address rather than a working warehouse, so it confirms the company's legal existence without confirming operational scale.

Third-party directory listing

Also listed on Peptide Supermarket

The homepage shows a badge for a listing on Peptide Supermarket, a third-party aggregator. That's a mild external signal worth noting but isn't a substitute for published testing documentation.

Editorial take

Real company, now with real documentation

PS Research Labs has a genuine, checkable UK company registration and, as of this update, a real per-product Certificate of Analysis link on every page checked, mostly through independent Janoshik verification. That's a meaningful improvement over this review's original findings, though the leftover "batch documentation available where applicable" copy and the one self-hosted PDF are worth a closer look before ordering.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

A stronger pick than before: worth considering for UK-based researchers who want a verifiable business registration plus a checkable COA, though it's still worth confirming the batch/lot number on the linked COA matches what actually ships.
