PS Research Labs review

PS Research Labs (trading as PS Labs) is a UK-registered supplier with a small catalog of common research compounds priced in GBP. The company is a real registered UK limited company with a listed registration number and registered office, which is a genuine trust signal, but the product pages themselves say little about testing: there's no named third-party lab, no published purity percentage, and the only documentation note reads "batch documentation available where applicable" rather than a firm commitment.

Why it stands out

Verifiable UK Company Registration

PS Research Labs Ltd lists a Companies House-style registration number (17327405) and a registered office address in Kington, Herefordshire, in its Terms of Service — details a reader can independently check, which is more concrete than many suppliers offer.

  • Small catalog: Retatrutide, Tirzepatide, BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, MOTS-C, and a few others at the time of this review
  • Prices shown publicly in GBP with no account required to browse
  • UK dispatch in 1-3 business days; international shipping available with customer responsible for customs
Buyer fit

Who this may suit

UK-based researchers who want a straightforward small catalog and value having a registered company name and number on file, and who don't need published lab documentation before ordering.

Review summary

Where it looks strongest

The registered company details are the clearest positive here — a real UK Ltd entity with a checkable registration number, which is more than a lot of storefronts disclose.

Trust signals

What's missing

No product page names a third-party testing lab, states a purity percentage, or links a sample Certificate of Analysis. The specifications section reads "Documentation: Batch documentation available where applicable," which doesn't commit to a CoA existing for any specific batch.

Potential drawback

What to double-check

Before ordering, ask directly whether a CoA is available for the specific batch and product being purchased, since it isn't published or referenced anywhere on the product pages themselves.

Ideal buyer

Who this page points toward

Someone prioritizing a verifiable UK business registration over published lab documentation, and comfortable requesting testing paperwork directly before buying.

Catalog and documentation

What's listed

The catalog covers a handful of common compounds — Retatrutide, Tirzepatide, BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, and MOTS-C were visible at the time of this review — each with a "Specifications" accordion listing strength, form, storage guidance, and the vague documentation note above.

Where COAs live

Not published, not named

There is no CoA library, no linked lab reports, and no named third-party testing partner anywhere on the site that this review could find. This is a meaningful gap compared with suppliers that publish batch-specific lab results.

Company details are real, but verify independently

Registration number 17327405

PS Research Labs Ltd's Terms of Service list a registered office at 61a Bridge Street, Kington, HR5 3DJ, United Kingdom. A registered office is a legal requirement for a UK Ltd company and can be a mail-forwarding address rather than a working warehouse, so it confirms the company's legal existence without confirming operational scale.

Third-party directory listing

Also listed on Peptide Supermarket

The homepage shows a badge for a listing on Peptide Supermarket, a third-party aggregator. That's a mild external signal worth noting but isn't a substitute for published testing documentation.

Editorial take

Real company, thin documentation

PS Research Labs has a genuine, checkable UK company registration, which is a real point in its favor, but the product pages don't name a testing lab, state a purity figure, or publish any CoA. Of the suppliers currently covered in this directory, it has one of the weaker documentation postures.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

Worth a look for UK-based researchers who value a verifiable business registration, but ask directly for batch-specific testing documentation before ordering rather than assuming it's included.
