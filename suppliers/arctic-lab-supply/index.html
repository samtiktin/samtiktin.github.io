Arctic Lab Supply review

By the PeptideSuppliers.org editorial team · How we review

Arctic Lab Supply is an Anchorage, Alaska research-peptide seller operated by Arctic Lab Supply LLC. Its strongest feature is documentation: a public library of lot-matched lab reports, each naming one product, strength and lot, with a link to the testing laboratory’s own verification page. It also publishes unusually specific shipping and refund terms. Its weaknesses are a limited number of reported products compared with the full catalog, and a documentation history that includes a September 2026 correction.

Testing and COAs in detail

What the library shows and what to do with it.

What is published

12 reports, 11 products

The library describes its reports as lot-matched and lists them with the product, lot, issuing laboratory, test date and reported result. Products include tesamorelin 10 mg, BPC-157, GHK-Cu, the KLOW and GLOW blends, retatrutide in two strengths, and two melanotan products. Reported purities in the table we read were in the high 99% range by HPLC.

The newest reports

Five from October 15, 2026

The site says five reports from Freedom Diagnostics (tesamorelin 10 mg, BPC-157 10 mg, GHK-Cu 100 mg, KLOW 80 mg, and a second independent report on retatrutide 10 mg, lot RT-2502-A) confirm identity by LC-MS and show two passing endotoxin replicates (LAL, USP <85>). Identity by mass spectrometry and endotoxin testing are more informative than a purity percentage alone.

Checking it

Use the laboratory’s page

Each row links to a verification page at the issuing laboratory. We did not independently verify the certificates, and several verification portals render with JavaScript. Open the link yourself, then match the lot, strength and date to the vial you receive.

A correction worth knowing about

Five older certificates were removed in September 2026

The site’s documentation corrections notes say five older certificate images were taken down in September 2026 after they were found to resolve to another vendor. Publishing a correction is better than leaving it up, and it is the reason we look closely at the verification links. It also means older screenshots or reposted certificates may not be reliable. Rely only on the current library.

A practical check before ordering

Four steps

  • Find your exact product and strength in the batch COA library
  • Open the laboratory link and confirm the lot, date and result match
  • Ask support whether the lot you are buying is the lot that was tested
  • On arrival, compare the label lot to the report

Shipping and policies

As stated on the site, checked October 31, 2026.

Shipping

Same-day dispatch claim and a guarantee

The shipping policy (updated October 25, 2026) says orders paid by midnight ET are packed and shipped the same day from Anchorage, with Sunday orders scanned by USPS on Monday, and that orders are guaranteed to ship by the next business day or shipping is refunded. The fine print adds a delivery guarantee: within 4 business days for Priority Mail or 3 for Priority Mail Express, or shipping back plus a $50 credit ($100 for Express). Read the exact conditions before relying on it. Shipping is by USPS.

Refunds

30 days on unopened product

The refund policy (updated October 10, 2026) offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on unopened products, with the buyer paying return shipping. Damaged, wrong or lost items get a free reship or full refund if reported within 48 hours of delivery, and orders can be cancelled for a full refund before shipping. International orders have a separate section covering customs and seizures.

What to watch

Points to weigh before ordering.

Coverage

Reports do not cover every product

Eleven products have a published report. For anything else, ask for documentation before buying and treat the absence of a report as an absence of evidence.

The company information page listed seven reports from ILS Laboratories, while the library listed twelve, including the newer Freedom Diagnostics reports. Trust the library and its dates.
