Testing and COAs in detail

What the library shows and what it does not.

What is published

About 60 reports across three laboratories

When we read the COA library, it held about 60 entries: roughly 31 from Bioregen, 21 from Bioviridian and 8 from Janoshik. The newest batch of 20 shared a test date of September 25, 2026, and covered products such as BPC-157, BPC-157 / TB-500, cagrilintide, epithalon, kisspeptin and pinealon. Purity figures ran from 99.0% to about 99.9% by the reports’ stated method.

Endotoxin

Reported for 21 entries

Twenty-one library rows show an endotoxin result, and those we read were under 0.05 EU/ml. Endotoxin testing is relevant to anything described as sterile, and a purity percentage does not replace it. For rows with no endotoxin result, ask whether one exists.

Loose ends

Details that need attention

The Bioviridian name appears with different spellings in the table, one entry has no test date, and the oldest reports are from July 2025. Reports are tied to the tested sample, so the date and lot matter. We could not tell whether each library row corresponds to the lot currently on sale.

A practical check before ordering

Four steps

  • Find your product and size in the library and note the laboratory and test date
  • Open the report and check the compound name, amount, lot and result
  • Ask support whether the report matches the lot you are buying
  • On arrival, compare the label lot to the report

Shipping and policies

As stated on the site, checked November 16, 2026.

Shipping

USPS from California

The shipping policy says orders ship within the United States only, by USPS, from a California warehouse. Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation, with First-Class Mail taking 3–5 business days and Priority Mail 1–3. Rates start at $5.99 and $8.99, and orders over $150 ship free. Non-US addresses are cancelled.

Returns

All sales are final

The policy states there are no refunds, returns or exchanges. You can cancel before an order ships by emailing immediately. For damaged or lost packages, contact them within 7 days of the expected delivery date with photos; a replacement may be offered at their discretion. This is stricter than suppliers that offer purity guarantees.

Support and legal

Stated scope

The footer lists an address at 501 W Broadway in San Diego and says NextGenPeps is not an outsourcing facility under section 503B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. The site says 24/7 support. Every page carries research-use-only wording.
