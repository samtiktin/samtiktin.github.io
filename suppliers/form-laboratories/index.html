Supplier review

Form Laboratories review

By the PeptideSuppliers.org editorial team · How we review

Form Laboratories is an online-only research-peptide supplier in Perth, Western Australia, operated by FORMA LABS PTY LTD. It is the first Australian supplier in our directory. It dispatches within Australia only, takes payment by bank transfer, and lists flat shipping and a 30-day purity guarantee. Its lab-report library is the weak point: the reports on file are sample reports from April 2026, most are heavy-metals screens, and every current lot is marked “not verified for listed lot.”

Why it matters

An Australian option, with candid report labelling

No other supplier in our directory is based in Australia, so Form Laboratories fills a gap for buyers there. Domestic dispatch from Perth also means parcels do not cross a border or pass through import inspection. To its credit, the site says plainly what each report does and does not cover.

  • Dispatches from Perth, WA via Australia Post or CouriersPlease, with tracking
  • Prices in AUD; flat $10 Standard and $20 Express shipping, free Standard from $250
  • A certificate library that states test type and says a sample report does not verify a different lot
  • 30-day purity guarantee and refunds for damaged orders
  • Strict research-use-only wording and no dosing guidance from support

Testing and reports in detail

What the certificate library shows.

What is published

Sample reports, not lot reports

The certificate library lists 12 current catalog lots. Ten have a sample report on file, all from sample lots coded FL20260410 (the example we saw was analysed April 21, 2026). Two, TB-500 and CJC-1295 (No DAC) & Ipamorelin, list “no verified sample report on file.” Reports are shown as images.

What was tested

Mostly heavy metals

Eight of the ten reports are heavy-metals analyses (a GHK-Cu example reported arsenic, cadmium, lead and mercury as not detected, via a named independent laboratory). Two, for tesamorelin and MOTS-C, are labelled “peptide vial assessment.” The site says itself that a heavy-metals screen does not measure purity or confirm identity.

Current lots

“Not verified for listed lot”

Every one of the 12 current lots carries the status “Not verified for listed lot.” The site explains that a historical report does not verify a different lot. That is accurate, but it means a buyer cannot currently match a vial to a report for its lot.

Inconsistent claims

Pages describe the documentation differently

The shop page says every listing has a lab report “confirming what it is and how pure it is,” and another page says every lot ships with a matched certificate. The home page, shipping page and returns page say certificates are available on request. The library shows sample reports and no verified lot match. Ask which statement applies to the lot you are buying.

The purity guarantee

Tied to a COA you may not be able to see

The returns page says the 30-day guarantee applies to the purity stated on the COA supplied with your order or available on request, and that a full refund follows if an independent lab retests the batch below it. Because the published reports mostly do not state purity, ask for the COA and its purity figure before ordering, and keep it.

A practical check before ordering

Five steps

  • Email hello@formlaboratories.com and ask for the report covering the lot you want, including purity and identity
  • Check the lot on the product page against the report’s lot, amount and test type
  • Ask who issued the report and for a reference you can verify with that laboratory
  • Keep the report and your order reference in case you need the purity guarantee
  • When the vial arrives, match its label lot to the report

Catalog and pricing

What was listed on November 3, 2026.

In stock

Retatrutide (10, 20 and 30 mg; from $149 AUD), GHK-Cu 50 mg ($79), TB-500 10 mg ($109), BPC-157 10 mg ($99), MOTS-C 10 mg ($99), Semax 11 mg ($89), and CJC-1295 (No DAC) & Ipamorelin 10 mg ($80). The BPC-157 page lists multi-vial savings of 10% for three vials and 18% for six.

Awaiting restock

Five listed but not stocked

Tesamorelin, NAD+, Selank, tirzepatide (listed as TRZ) and the BPC-157 & TB-500 blend showed “Not currently stocked” with an invoice-request option. The submission to us named tesamorelin, NAD+ and selank, so check availability before you plan an order around them.

Extra content

Research guides and a stack tool

The site has research and learn pages per compound. It also links a “Build your stack” tool on a separate subdomain, which we did not review. Dosing and preparation guidance is outside what we publish, and the supplier says its support does not advise on use.

Shipping and policies

As stated on the site, checked November 3, 2026.

Ordering and payment

Invoice first, then bank transfer

Checkout does not take a card. You place an order, receive an emailed invoice with bank details, and pay by transfer; PayID and Osko are supported. The order sits as awaiting payment until the transfer clears and is matched, and dispatch follows. If you prefer card protection, this is a real trade-off.

Shipping

Flat rates, domestic only

Standard is $10 (5–7 days) and Express Post $20 (2–3 business days), counted from dispatch, with free Standard on orders of $250 or more. Parcels are plain and tracked. There is no international shipping and no local pickup. The site quotes the same national windows for every state and says tracking gives the real estimate.

Returns

Damage and purity, not change of mind

Damaged-on-arrival orders are refunded if you keep the packaging and label and email photos with the order reference. The 30-day purity guarantee is described above. Vials cannot be returned for change of mind once shipped, though the page says Australian Consumer Law rights that cannot be excluded still apply.

Company

FORMA LABS PTY LTD

Form Laboratories’ message gave its legal name as FORMA LABS PTY LTD with an ABN, based in Perth WA 6000. The site describes an online-only business with no shopfront or collection counter. Support is by email at hello@formlaboratories.com. We did not see named staff or a street address on the pages we read.

Legal framing

Research-use-only, and the site says it is not a defence

Every page carries research-use-only wording and says buyers must be 21 or over. The homepage also states that Australian and WA law apply to each substance and that a research-use-only label does not make supply lawful. This review does not assess the legality of any product; check the rules where you live.

Editorial take

Useful for Australian buyers, with documentation still to prove

Form Laboratories offers clear shipping terms, domestic dispatch and candid wording about what its reports cover. It earns a middling rating because the library holds sample reports rather than lot-matched results, most are heavy-metals screens that do not show purity or identity, and the purity guarantee depends on a COA that buyers may need to request. We are rating documentation and buyer risk, not product quality, because we have not tested product.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

Reasonable to consider if you ask for the exact-lot COA with purity and identity first, and you are comfortable with bank transfer. Compare it with Form Laboratories’s own certificate page before you order.
