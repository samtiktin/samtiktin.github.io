Supplier review

Vorix Labs review

Vorix Labs is the research-peptide storefront of Brij Technologies LLC, a USA-based supplier shipping from a domestic warehouse. Every active catalog listing ships with a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis from Janoshik Analytical, and the site publishes a browsable CoA library where each PDF links out to an independent verification page on Janoshik's own site — not just a document Vorix hosts itself. Pricing is public, checkout is standard card, and new customers get 10% off plus a free bacteriostatic water vial with code BAC.

Why it stands out

Independently Verifiable COAs

Vorix Labs states plainly that it does not make its own purity claims — every active vial ships with a Janoshik Analytical CoA, and the public library links each one to a live verification page on Janoshik's own site where a reader can cross-check the batch number independent of Vorix.

  • 14 batch-specific CoAs published at the time of this review, covering BPC-157/TB-500, GHK-Cu, GLP-3 (retatrutide), Tesamorelin, and more
  • USA warehouse, standard card checkout, public pricing with no account required to browse
  • Damage-protection policy: one free replacement per order for verified transit damage, photo evidence required
Buyer fit

Who this may suit

Researchers who want to check a specific batch's lab result against an independent source before ordering, rather than taking a supplier's own PDF at face value.

Review summary

Where it looks strongest

The CoA library is the standout: every PDF is the same one emailed after purchase, posted publicly with no login wall, and most link to a Janoshik verification URL that isn't controlled by Vorix. That's a meaningfully stronger documentation posture than a supplier that only says COAs are "available."

Trust signals

What the page communicates clearly

A named operating entity (Brij Technologies LLC), a named third-party lab (Janoshik Analytical), public pricing, standard card checkout, and a documented damage-replacement process with clear eligibility rules.

Potential drawback

What to double-check

The damage-protection policy is replacement-only for verified transit damage — it explicitly does not cover change-of-mind or dissatisfaction, and only one replacement is allowed per order, so treat it as shipping insurance rather than a general return policy.

Ideal buyer

Who this page points toward

Someone who wants to compare a specific compound's identity-purity documentation across suppliers and values being able to check that documentation against an independent source.

Catalog and documentation

What's listed

The catalog spans common research compounds including BPC-157/TB-500, CJC-1295/Ipamorelin, GHK-Cu, KPV, MOTS-c, NAD+, SS-31, Selank, Semax, Tesamorelin, Melanotan-II, and GLP-3/GLP-2 (retatrutide/tirzepatide) in multiple strengths, plus bacteriostatic water. Bundles are grouped by research category (mitochondrial, growth hormone, metabolic).

Where COAs live

Published library, independently checkable

The CoA library lists each active batch with a preview image, a downloadable PDF, and in most cases a "Verify on Janoshik" link to Janoshik's own results page for that specific test ID — a stronger setup than a supplier-hosted PDF alone, since the underlying lab result isn't only available through the seller.

Replacement policy is narrow

Transit damage only, one per order

Read the Returns & Refunds page before ordering: eligible claims cover shipping damage, identity-purity shortfalls, and wrong items, each requiring photo evidence and a report within a reasonable window. Reconstituted products and claims without proof of purchase are explicitly excluded, and there's no general change-of-mind return.

Community space is gated

Discord requires verification

Vorix runs an invite-only Discord for verified-account holders, moderated for RUO compliance. It's a reasonable way to keep dosing-advice content out of a public channel, but it means the community layer isn't open the way the CoA library is.

Editorial take

One of the more independently verifiable listings in this directory

Vorix Labs pairs a named third-party lab with a public library of batch-specific results that link out to that lab's own verification pages — a documentation standard most suppliers here only partially match. Combined with public pricing, standard checkout, and a clearly scoped replacement policy, it's a strong fit for readers who want to check the paperwork themselves.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

A strong option for documentation-focused buyers. Just remember the replacement policy covers verified transit damage only, not general returns, and the Discord community requires account verification to join.
