Peptides.com review

Peptides.com is a multi-brand RUO marketplace rather than a single house label, hosting several in-house brands including Real Peptides, Catalyst Compounds, Groov Bioscience, NuLumin Bio-Sciences, Novelle Bio, and Verified Peptides under one storefront. Prices are listed publicly on every product page, and each listing links a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis with an analysis date, with no account required to view either. Checkout runs through standard card payment (Visa, Amex, Discover, JCB, Diners) rather than crypto or bank transfer, and shipping is US-only via USPS or UPS.

Why it stands out

Public Pricing and Per-Batch COAs

Peptides.com publishes prices and lot-specific COAs directly on each product page, without gating either behind an account. That combination is more transparent than most suppliers in this directory, where pricing, testing documents, or both typically require sign-up first.

  • Six in-house brands sold under one cart: Real Peptides, Catalyst Compounds, Groov Bioscience, NuLumin Bio-Sciences, Novelle Bio, and Verified Peptides
  • Public prices from about $33 for single-compound vials up to $190+ for larger multi-compound blends
  • US-only shipping via USPS or UPS, 3–6 business day processing, with optional Route package protection at checkout
Buyer fit

Who this may suit

Researchers who want to compare price and COA side by side before adding an item to cart, and who prefer a standard card checkout over crypto or manual payment review.

Review summary

Where it looks strongest

Documentation transparency is the standout. Every product page checked for this review listed a public price alongside a linked COA image showing a lot number and analysis date, viewable without creating an account.

Trust signals

What the page communicates clearly

Standard card processing (Visa, Amex, Discover, JCB, Diners) and an optional third-party Route shipping-protection add-on are both disclosed at checkout, rather than the crypto-only or pay-after-review flows seen on some suppliers covered here.

Potential drawback

What to double-check

Like most RUO suppliers, all sales are final except for fulfillment errors reported within 7 days of delivery, and lost or stolen packages are only covered if Route protection was purchased at checkout — it's added by default, so check the cart before completing an order.

Ideal buyer

Who this page points toward

Someone comparing several house brands under one cart who wants to check a batch's COA before ordering rather than after, and who is comfortable with a standard, all-sales-final RUO checkout.

Catalog and documentation

What's listed

The catalog spans common compounds like BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, Semax, and NAD+ alongside blends such as GLOW, KLOW, and the Wolverine Blend, sold under whichever in-house brand carries that listing. A dedicated peptide index and per-brand shop pages make it straightforward to compare the same compound across brands.

Where COAs live

Published per product, organized by brand

A Lab Reports hub organizes COAs by brand (Catalyst Compounds, Groov Bioscience, Novelle Bio, NuLumin Bio-Sciences, Real Peptides), and individual product pages also link a lot-specific COA image with an analysis date directly beneath the listing — a public COA library rather than a request-only or account-gated one.

Checkout terms deserve a read

All sales final, with opt-out package protection

The refund policy states all sales are final except for missing, incorrect, or defective items reported within 7 days of delivery. Route shipping protection is added to the cart automatically; a reader who skips it and later has a package lost, stolen, or damaged in transit has no fallback coverage from Peptides.com directly.

Business address on file

Registered address, not a storefront

Policy pages list a Cheyenne, Wyoming suite address, which reads as a registered-agent or mail-forwarding address typical of an online-only seller rather than a physical storefront. That's common across this directory and isn't unusual on its own, but it's not a walk-in location.

Editorial take

One of the more transparent listings in this directory

Peptides.com pairs public pricing with public, lot-specific COAs and a standard card checkout — a combination most suppliers here only partially match. The multi-brand structure also gives readers more compounds and formats to compare without leaving one cart.

Bottom line

Quick verdict

A strong option for readers who want to verify pricing and testing documentation before ordering. Just confirm the Route protection add-on at checkout, since it's the only path to a replacement if a package goes missing in transit.
