Documentation checklist

What to look for on supplier pages

Austin readers should lead with supplier policy clarity, refund and contact terms specifically, then check COA access, batch details, and research-use labeling.

  • COA references should be easy to find, readable, and tied to a specific batch rather than a general testing claim.
  • Batch-testing language is strongest when it connects a batch identifier to a current, independently hosted lab document.
  • Shipping language should explain dispatch timing and carrier visibility without making unverifiable speed or quality claims.
  • Research-use labeling should stay consistent across the supplier's directory listing, profile page, and policy pages.
