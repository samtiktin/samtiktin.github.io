Before you post

This is a research-use-only discussion space

Keep discussion focused on laboratory research use: sourcing, documentation, testing, and supplier transparency. This is not the place for dosing advice, human-use instructions, or medical guidance — see the disclosure page for the site's full RUO position.

Good first topics

What people usually post about

  • Reading and comparing COAs across suppliers
  • Shipping timelines, packaging, and tracking experiences
  • Questions about a specific listing in the peptide directory or supplier hub
  • Flagging a supplier that's changed pricing, gone offline, or stopped shipping
How it works

Hosted on GitHub Discussions

The forum lives on GitHub rather than this domain, which keeps hosting and spam moderation free and reliable. Sign in with a GitHub account, pick a category, and start or join a thread. Existing threads are visible to everyone, no account required to read.

Account: Free GitHub sign-in to post Reading: Open to everyone
Ground rules

Keep it useful and on topic

Share documentation, ask direct questions, and correct outdated information when you see it. Avoid posting content that promotes human or veterinary use, and don't share information that could be used to circumvent a supplier's compliance or verification steps.

Something off in the directory?

Flag it directly too

Forum threads are a good place to crowdsource opinions, but for corrections you want reflected in a review or listing, also send a note through the contact page so it gets tracked for an update.
